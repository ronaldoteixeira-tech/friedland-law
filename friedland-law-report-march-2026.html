06 · Action Plan

April 2026 — Optimization Sprint

Three parallel workstreams: media quality, Jacksonville acceleration, and digital asset consolidation.

Media & Lead Quality
  • 🚫
    Shut Down WhatsApp Lead Campaigns
    Immediate deactivation of Meta campaigns driving WhatsApp as primary CTA. Zero-friction entry points are generating unqualified volume that wastes Intake capacity.
  • 📋
    Launch Gated Native Lead Forms (Meta)
    Deploy forms with hard qualification fields: accident date, injury presence, liability clarity. Only leads passing minimum criteria proceed to contact.
  • 🎯
    Accelerate Jacksonville Bids
    Raise average CPCs on long-tail Jacksonville terms using ~$3k in Q1 carryover budget. Target competitive positioning against Farah & Farah in the region.
Digital Assets
  • 📍
    Google Business Profile — Content Takeover
    Agency assumes all GBP posting. Evaluating Sprout Social for simultaneous publishing across GBP and social channels (Meta Business Suite lacks GBP support).
  • 🌐
    Website Structural Completion
    Blog infrastructure, animations, and resolution of 81 flagged technical SEO errors. Scope: addendum contract at $3,000 one-time payment.
  • 📊
    Intake Script Revision
    Collaborate with Intake team to redesign call script around case-viability checkpoints. Align marketing qualification questions with attorney intake criteria.
