⚠ Critical — Sem Visão de Avanço dos Leads
We can see leads enter Intaker and GoHighLevel, but we have no visibility into what happens next — whether a lead was contacted, qualified, converted into a signed case, or lost. Friedland's own documented CRM pipeline (New Potential Client → Qualified Lead → Support Information Gathering → DocuSign Signed) exists inside GoHighLevel, but its stage data isn't shared with the agency. Combined with the fact that 83% of Google's 122 tracked conversions are phone calls — which may not consistently get logged as leads in either system — the real gap between "what Google says converted" and "what actually became a qualified case" is unmeasured. Without stage-level access we cannot report true cost-per-qualified-lead or cost-per-case, only cost-per-ad-conversion.
